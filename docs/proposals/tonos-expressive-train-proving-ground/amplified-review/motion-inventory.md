This inventory covers the preview-only Train Overview, Train Plans, and shared bottom-navigation slice. Motion wraps existing Tonos widgets; selection, workout/plan data, navigation, and callbacks remain owned by their current state and routes.

## Shared rules

- The press/release spring is a standard Flutter spring: mass 1, stiffness 800, damping ratio 0.72. The selector/navigation spring uses stiffness 650 and damping ratio 0.80.
- Press and selection callbacks update state immediately. Animation never delays business or navigation state.
- The wrappers preserve child layout, hit testing, keyboard activation, and semantics; transforms are paint-only.
- One-shot controllers are disposed with their widgets. The ambient controller is lifecycle-aware and has no delayed callbacks or repeating timers.
- Effects Off uses the preview's existing `MediaQuery.disableAnimations` path. It changes motion, not the Expressive palette or geometry.
- This slice adds no motion to Active Workout, Catalog, Logbook, Progress, Profile, Nutrition, or settings screens.
