Privacy Policy for Tonos - Health and Fitness

Last updated: August 12, 2026

Tonos - Health and Fitness is a local-first fitness, nutrition, and body metrics app. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

Information We Collect

Tonos stores workout, nutrition, body metric, profile, and app preference information locally on your device so the app can provide its features. Tonos does not sell this information or use it for advertising.

Camera Access

Tonos may request camera access for barcode scanning in nutrition features. Camera input is used to detect barcode values on your device. Camera images or video are not uploaded, sold, or shared by Tonos.

How Information Is Used

Information you enter into the app is used only to provide local app functionality, such as logging workouts, tracking nutrition, displaying progress, saving preferences, and matching scanned or entered barcodes to food records available in the app.

Data Sharing

Tonos downloads public exercise and anatomy media from the configured content service. These requests do not include your workout, nutrition, body metric, or profile database. The app does not include advertising or behavioural analytics.

Optional Anonymous Diagnostics

Current production builds do not send remote diagnostics. The Share anonymous diagnostics control is unavailable unless a build has been deliberately configured with Tonos' approved diagnostics relay.

If Tonos later enables that relay, it will receive only an app version, build number, platform, approved fault or sync category, outcome, and coarse duration and item-count ranges. It will never receive error messages, stack traces, names, health data, database contents, screenshots, network addresses, usage analytics, or a device identifier. Tonos will not forward these reports to Sentry or another diagnostics vendor.

Cloudflare would process the connection to operate the relay. Tonos will not store client IP addresses, headers, user agents, location, or request bodies in the relay database or application logs. Submitted primary rows would expire after 14 days. Cloudflare D1 recovery history can retain a deleted row for up to 30 additional days.

Content-sync diagnostic history remains local to your device. It contains only the operation, source category, outcome, duration, manifest version, item count, and redacted error type. It never includes workout, nutrition, body metric, profile, or database contents.

Data Storage, Security, and Retention

Your app data is stored locally on your device. It remains on your device until you delete it in the app, clear the app's data through your device settings, or uninstall the app. On Android, Tonos excludes its database and preferences from automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Your health data leaves the app only when you deliberately create an export.

Database exports are unencrypted JSON files. They can include workouts, nutrition, body metrics, profile information, and preferences. Tonos warns before creating an export; you are responsible for saving it only to storage and providers you trust. Current exports do not use an app-managed encryption password.

The app keeps at most 30 content-sync diagnostic entries on the device. You can clear them from Diagnostics & Privacy. Current production builds have no remote diagnostics retention because no relay endpoint is enabled. Any future relay row will have a 14-day primary retention period, with up to 30 additional days of provider-managed recovery history after expiry or deletion.

Deleting Your Data

See the Tonos Data Deletion Guide for steps covering sync history, local app data, exported backups, cached media, and any future anonymous diagnostics shared through the relay.

Children's Privacy

Tonos is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
