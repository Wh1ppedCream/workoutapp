Here’s a condensed, high‑level overview that preserves key architecture and UI details in as few words as possible:

---

## 1. App Entry & State

* **`main.dart`**:

  * Wraps `MyApp` in `MultiProvider` for `ActiveSession`, `SelectedProfile`, `DashboardConfig` (persisted via SharedPreferences).
  * `MyApp` uses a `FutureBuilder` on `onboardingComplete` to show `OnboardingFlow` or `MainScreen`.
* **`MainScreen`**:

  * 5‑tab `BottomNavigationBar` (Dashboard, Train, History, Nutrition, Profile).
  * FAB shows `OngoingSessionFab` when a session is active.

---

## 2. Onboarding Flow

* Multi‑page setup (`PageController`): Welcome → Personal Info → Usage Intent → optionally Anthropometry, Weight History, Activity, Nutrition & Training Preferences → Summary.
* Fields: name, gender, DOB, height, weight, calories/goals, diet/program type.
* Validation on each page; “Skip/Finish” and “Next” buttons.

---

## 3. Dashboard Page

* **View Mode**: list of enabled widgets in user‑defined order.
* **Edit Mode**: drag‑reorder `ReorderableListView`, toggle visibility, “Add Widget” dialog.
* Widgets include QuickBar, NutritionDash, WorkoutDashboard, HistorySummary, PastSessionsList, DataRecords, HealthTrends, CurrentMetrics.

---

## 4. Train & Presets

* **TrainPage**: left drawer, right profile drawer, list of presets (`PresetBar`), “Generate” & “Add Preset” actions, “New Session” button.
* **PresetDetailScreen**: view/edit exercise list, toggle automatic, edit name/flow, start session.
* **Auto‑Preset Flow**: graph editor with `flutter_flow_chart`, manage methods/events.

---

## 5. History & Analytics

* **HistoryScreen**: buttons to Exercises/Muscle filters, `HistorySummaryWidget`, “7‑Day Analytics” navigates to `AnalyticsDashboardScreen`.
* **SessionDetailScreen**: list of exercises (`ExerciseCard`), edit/delete, save changes.
* **AnalyticsDashboardScreen**: tabbed view of sets by muscle/body‑part over last 7 days.

---

## 6. Exercise Catalog & Filters

* **ExerciseCatalogPage**: search, filter dialog (equipment, area, muscle, profile), list of definitions, detail bottom sheet with tabs (Details, Metrics, Records).
* **DefinitionsByBodyPart/Muscle**: list exercises grouped by body‑part or muscle.

---

## 7. Nutrition & Food Logging

* **NutritionPage**: scrollable column of `NutritionDash`, HealthTrendsSection, DataRecordsSection, CurrentMetricsSection; `SpeedDialFab`.
* **FoodLoggingPage**: tabs (Scan/Search/Pre‑Planned/Custom), search results list, add‑food flow to `FoodCustomizationPage`.
* **FoodCustomizationPage**: form for name, brand, images, macros + breakdown expansions.

---

## 8. Measurements & Logs

* **MeasuredItemsPage** → **NewMeasurementItemPage** → **SpecificMeasurementPage**: list of tracked measurements, presets/custom inputs, charts with filters, add new entries.

---

## 9. Profile & Settings

* **ProfilePage**: unit toggles, reminders, body‑part mapping, list of gym profiles (add/edit).
* **AppSettingsPage**: export/import DB JSON.
* **AnalyticsSettingsScreen**: navigation to body‑part/muscle mapping, ranking, percent‑per‑exercise, volume boundaries.
* **FlowChartPage**: demo tree editor.

---

## 10. Data Models

* **workout\_models.dart**: sessions, weight/cardio/stretch exercises, sets/instances.
* **definition\_models.dart**: ExerciseDefinition, Equipment, BodyPart, Muscle.
* **measurement\_models.dart**: definitions & entries.
* **gym\_models.dart**: GymProfile + equipment map.
* **preset\_models.dart**: presets, flow definitions/methods/edges.
* **analytics\_models.dart**: RepMaxRow, VolumeBoundaries.
