Here’s a streamlined widget reference, highlighting purpose and key structure for each custom component:

---

### QuickBar

• **What:** Three‑segment action bar (Measurement, Food, Workout)
• **Key Props:** `scale`
• **Structure:** Rounded `Container` → `Row` of three `InkWell` segments (containers with background colors, labels scaled) separated by dividers
• **Actions:** Pushes measurement, food, or workout screens via `Navigator`/`ActiveSession`

---

### NutritionDash

• **What:** Swipeable nutrition views + pager dots + add‑meal bar
• **Key Props:** nutrient ints, `scale`
• **Structure:** `PageView` of `[NutritionCircleDetails, NutritionBarDetails, NutritionTextDetails]` inside fixed‑height box, animated dot indicators, `MealPlanAddBar`

---

### NutritionTextDetails & ValueCard

• **What:** Text overview of calories/macros in labeled cards
• **Key Props (TextDetails):** nutrient ints, `scale`
• **ValueCard Props:** `label`, `value`, optional `borderColor`, `scale`
• **Structure:** Rows of `Expanded ValueCard` showing “Remaining/Consumed/Target” and “Protein/Carbs/Fat”

---

### RoundedProgressIndicator

• **What:** Circular progress with rounded ends
• **Key Props:** `progress` (0–1), `size`, `progressColor`, `backgroundColor`, `strokeWidth`, `scale`
• **Structure:** `CustomPaint` draws background circle + arc with `strokeCap.round`

---

### WorkoutDashboard

• **What:** Profile selector, preset list, “Start Workout” button
• **Key Props:** `scale`
• **Structure:** `DropdownButtonFormField` for profiles; `FutureBuilder` list of `PresetBar`; bottom large button to kick off session

---

### PresetBar

• **What:** Colored bar for a workout preset with menu & auto badge
• **Key Props:** `presetId`, `label`, `color`, `isAutomatic`, `scale`
• **Structure:** `GenericBar` background, label tap → detail screen, trailing row with auto icon + `PopupMenuButton` (Edit/Rename/Delete)

---

### PastSessionsList

• **What:** Filterable carousel of recent sessions
• **Key Props:** `height`, `onReload`
• **Structure:** `Card` with header (filter dropdown + fullscreen icon) + `FutureBuilder` list of session cards → detail screen on tap

---

### OngoingSessionFab

• **What:** Toggles between single FAB and resume/exit actions for active session
• **Key Props:** —
• **Structure:** Stateful switch: compact green FAB vs. row of two extended FABs (Resume, Exit) with confirmation

---

### SessionCompleteSheet

• **What:** Draggable summary of just‑finished workout
• **Key Props:** `sessionId`
• **Structure:** `FutureBuilder` loads session+exercises → `DraggableScrollableSheet` with header (date, duration, volume, calories, score) and scrollable list of each exercise summary; close FAB

---

### SpeedDialFab

• **What:** Expanding FAB to log food or measurements
• **Key Props:** —
• **Structure:** Main FAB toggles two extended FABs (“Log Food”, “Log Measurement”) with animated rotation

---

### StretchSearchDialog

• **What:** Dialog to pick a stretch by body part
• **Flow:** Body‑part dropdown → fetch stretches → stretch dropdown → “Add”/“Cancel”

---

### StretchCard

• **What:** Manage a stretch exercise’s instances
• **Key Props:** `exercise`, `readOnlyMode`, callbacks
• **Structure:** Card with header menu, search/custom add row, list of checkable instances with delete icons

---

### WeightCard

• **What:** Edit weight exercise sets (and optional change‑sets)
• **Key Props:** `exercise`, `readOnlyMode`, callbacks
• **Structure:** Card with header (name, note edit, info/details, menu), parent sets list (checkbox, weight/reps fields, remove), optional change‑set section, “Add Set” button

---

Each widget emphasizes a clear, scaled layout, consistent padding, and callback hooks for navigation or state updates.
